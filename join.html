Postdoctoral Associate — Discrete Global Grid Systems (DGGS)

The GATOR Lab at the University of Florida is seeking a full-time Postdoctoral Associate to conduct foundational or computational research on Discrete Global Grid Systems (DGGS). We offer high autonomy for an ambitious researcher to define and lead a project at the intersection of modern computing and geospatial science.

📄 Download the Postdoc Position Flyer (PDF)

Research Directions

We welcome candidates with either a theoretical/methodological focus or a computational/systems engineering background. You may propose work along either track or bridge both:

  • Track A: Theoretical & Methodological Foundations
    Investigating spatial aggregation effects, geometric and areal distortion, aperture and nesting properties across tessellations (hexagonal, pentagonal, triangular), spatial statistics on discrete grids, or cross-resolution topology.
  • Track B: Computational Systems & Open-Source Tooling
    Developing DGGS benchmarks, multi-grid interoperability, scalable indexing algorithms, or native integration with cloud-native columnar ecosystems (e.g., GeoParquet, DuckDB, Arrow, STAC) and GeoAI workflows.

Appointment Terms, Requirements & Resources

  • Duration: 1-year initial appointment, extendable based on satisfactory performance and funding availability.
  • Start Date: As soon as feasible.
  • Salary: $60,000 – $70,000.
  • Requirements: Ph.D. in Geomatics, GIScience, Computer Science, Geography, or a related quantitative discipline.
  • Location: Based at the Fort Lauderdale Research and Education Center in the Miami metropolitan area.
  • Compute Infrastructure: Dedicated high-performance in-lab GPU workstations plus direct access to HiPerGator, one of the most powerful AI supercomputers in U.S. academia.
  • Career Launchpad: Direct mentorship, opportunities to co-mentor PhD students, full support for publishing high-impact papers and open-source software, and conference travel support.

How to Apply (Postdoc)

Applications are reviewed on an ongoing basis. Please email Dr. Levente Juhász at with DGGS-POSTDOC as the subject line prefix and include:

  1. CV and links to your GitHub / software portfolio or Google Scholar.
  2. A 1-Page Research Pitch: Skip the generic cover letter and do not write a long proposal—send a concise (1-page max) pitch outlining a specific DGGS problem or general direction you want to work on in the next year(s), why it matters, and expected outputs.
  3. Name and contact information of at least 2 references familiar with you.

The University of Florida is a Top 10 U.S. public university and an Equal Opportunity Institution. Final hiring is contingent upon formal application via UF Careers and background/immigration clearance.

Funded PhD Student (Fall 2027)

The GATOR Lab is seeking a PhD student for a 4-year Graduate Research Assistantship (ideal start date: Fall 2027, filled only if a suitable candidate is found). As a newly established lab at the intersection of modern computing and geospatial science, we offer the unique opportunity to be part of something new and help shape the future direction of the research group.

📄 Download the PhD Position Flyer (PDF)

Research Focus Areas

  1. GeoAI & Scalable Analytics
    Developing next-generation GeoAI frameworks and cloud-native workflows. Research topics may include Large Language Models (LLMs) for geospatial reasoning, Agentic AI, and scalable geocomputation frameworks.
  2. Discrete Global Grid Systems (DGGS)
    Foundational research into one of the most exciting geospatial topics of recent times, focusing on spatial indexing, multi-resolution analysis, and global-scale data integration.

We also welcome original, well-motivated research proposals from well-qualified candidates whose interests align with our core pillars. To be considered, you will need to articulate what you will bring to the table and why you want to work with us.

Assistantship Details & Resources

  • Assistantship Package: 4-year research assistantship including an annual stipend, full tuition waiver, and health insurance.
  • Compute & Mentorship: Access to our dedicated in-lab GPU workstation, HiPerGator, and close personal mentorship in a collaborative environment.
  • Location: Based at the Fort Lauderdale Research and Education Center (near Miami). Students may be based in Gainesville, FL during their first year to complete coursework.

How to Apply (PhD)

  • Priority Deadline: December 31, 2026 (applications are reviewed on a rolling basis).
  • Please email Dr. Levente Juhász at with:
    1. Your CV and a link to your portfolio or GitHub (if available).
    2. A Brief Statement of Interest explaining why you want to join the GATOR Lab and how your interests align with our research pillars. Be creative, authentic, and tell us why you want to join.

Published papers are nice to have, but not mandatory. Exceptional candidates may be nominated for additional fellowships and external recognition. Final admission depends on satisfying all UF Graduate Admission and U.S. immigration requirements (for international candidates).

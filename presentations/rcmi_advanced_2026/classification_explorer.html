Quantile
Equal number of features per class. Most common in public health.
Equal Interval
Equal-width value ranges. Can produce empty classes with skewed data.
Natural Breaks (Jenks)
Minimizes within-class variance. Finds "natural" groupings in data.
Standard Deviation
Classes based on distance from the mean. Best for normally distributed data.
